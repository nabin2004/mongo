Deep Dive Concepts

Indexes & Execution Stats

The Plain-English Breakdown: Indexes make queries faster by ordering fields (B-Tree). .explain('executionStats') shows how the query was processed (COLLSCAN vs IXSCAN).

Real-World Use Case: Optimizing a slow query that searches by email or username.

The "Gotcha" (Common Pitfalls): Indexes speed up reads but slow down writes (inserts/updates/deletes) because the index must be updated too. Don't index every field.

Memorization Hook: COLLSCAN = Bad (reads everything). IXSCAN = Good (reads index).

Examiner: What is a COLLSCAN and why is it problematic?
Collection Scan. It means the database had to look at every single document to find the result, which is extremely slow on large datasets.
Examiner: How do you enforce uniqueness on a field?
Create a unique index: db.collection.createIndex({email: 1}, {unique: true})
